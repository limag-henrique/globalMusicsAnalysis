## Method and qualification

ORICON BiZ describes a current annual Streaming TOP100 product, but that advertised product depth is not year-specific public table evidence. Its current weekly Streaming description discusses provider-supplied playback counts across paid and ad-supported services; it does not establish the historical provider roster or formula for each annual ranking. The per-year weighting and aggregation formula therefore remain unknown. The 2018 annual period ended before the first weekly Streaming issue identified by the service menu, and no 2018 annual Streaming table was found. [ORICON BiZ menu](https://biz.oricon.co.jp/menu.asp), [current weekly Streaming method](https://www.oricon.co.jp/rank/st/w/2025-12-15/), [2018 annual page](https://www.oricon.co.jp/confidence/special/52268/5/), and [2018 period article](https://www.oricon.co.jp/news/2125862/full/).

The public 2019–2025 pages did not request login or payment. No subscriber table was opened or purchased. Eight serial unauthenticated first-party page reads were made for the 2019–2025 pages; the access tool returned rendered HTML text but did not expose response status or raw byte counts. Earlier raw captures and rendered extracts were preserved unchanged from the review packet. The new packet is [here](../data/runtime/oricon-jp-annual-streaming-2018-2025-acquisition-20261118-01/), including per-year accounting, retrieval records, copied evidence, and checksums.

This remains a distinct ORICON provider series and must not be mixed with Spotify, YouTube, Billboard JAPAN, Apple Music, ORICON Digital Single, physical Single, or the separate release-year-only ranking.
