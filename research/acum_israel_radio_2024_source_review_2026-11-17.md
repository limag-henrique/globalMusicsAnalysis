# ACUM Israel radio 2024: source review

Audit date: 2026-11-17.

## Primary sources and supported claim

ACUM's official year-end news archive lists “אקו״ם מסכמת את 2024 במוסיקה” (“ACUM sums up 2024 in music”) with a publication date of **2025-01-01**. Its excerpt says ACUM presents the songs and musicians most played on Israeli radio stations in 2024 and names “גיבורי על” by Hatikva 6 as the most-played Israeli song.

The official [2024 annual summary PDF](https://acum.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ANN-RE-24-HEB.pdf), titled “דוח סיכום שנתי 2024,” is hosted under `/2025/10/` (the PDF gives no separate publication day). It makes the period and measurement context explicit on printed pages **44–45**, in its ACUM ON AIR section. It describes a system that monitors radio broadcasts and identifies works, then states: “השיר הישראלי המושמע ביותר בשנת 2024 לפי ACUM ON AIR ‘גיבורי על’ בביצוע התקווה 6” — “The most-played Israeli song in 2024 according to ACUM ON AIR was ‘Superheroes,’ performed by Hatikva 6.”

This supports a **rank-1 equivalent among Israeli songs** for calendar year 2024, derived from ACUM's “most-played Israeli song” superlative. The report does not print a numeric rank or identify this as rank 1 among all songs, regardless of origin.

Primary-source links: [ACUM's news archive, page 2](https://acum.org.il/category/%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA/page/2/), [the year-end article page](https://acum.org.il/%D7%90%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%9B%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%AA-2024-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94/), and [ACUM's annual reports page](https://acum.org.il/%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D/). ACUM's [2025 awards winners page](https://acum.org.il/creators/awards/winners/2025-2024) also lists “גיבורי על” by Hatikva 6 as the most-played Israeli work in its ACUM ON AIR section; this is corroboration, while the award page's 2025 label is not the chart period.

## Full-list availability and limitations

I found no complete ACUM 2024 radio song table or any rank-2-and-below rows in the accessible first-party materials. The official news archive provides only a short excerpt. The linked article page currently serves a generic site shell after ACUM's website migration, so its full article content and any additional rows are unavailable there. The report supplies no play counts, complete chart depth, or station count alongside this claim. Do not fill in later ranks from other publishers as ACUM rows.

The report and article concern **calendar year 2024**: the report explicitly says “בשנת 2024” (“in 2024”), and the year-end article appears in the January 2025 archive. Keep this period distinct from the separate ACUM awards cycle labeled 2025.

One misleading URL is [annual-report-sh-24.pdf](https://acum.org.il/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/annual-report-sh-24.pdf): despite the `/2024/11/` upload path, its cover and content identify it as ACUM's **2023** annual report. It is not the source for the 2024 claim.
