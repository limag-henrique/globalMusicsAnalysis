## Method and scope

The ORICON annual pages identify a Streaming Ranking and print play counts for the ranked songs in 2019–2024. They state their year-specific periods, but do not state an annual aggregation formula, weighting, or tie policy. The 2025 article provides a collective top-four statement without a song-by-song table.

ORICON BiZ currently advertises annual Streaming TOP100 and says its digital rankings are based on service sales results without nationwide estimation. That is general service information, not evidence that each historical annual page published or captured a complete 100-row table. ORICON’s current weekly Streaming method page describes play counts supplied by services for audio or music-video playback, including paid on-demand and ad-supported plays; it does not establish an unchanged historical annual method. ([ORICON BiZ](https://biz.oricon.co.jp/menu.asp) · [current weekly method page](https://www.oricon.co.jp/rank/st/w/2025-12-15/))

## Packet and limitations

The [reserved acquisition packet](../data/runtime/oricon-jp-annual-streaming-2018-2025-acquisition-20261118-01/) contains one independent series record per year, 100 individually ranked rows for 2019–2024, four collective 2025 observations with no individual ranks assigned, source captures, request log, copied prior-packet evidence, reservation copy, and checksums. The inherited raw HTML captures for 2019–2020 were reused. Official 2021–2025 pages were opened serially and saved as concise rendered-text extracts; the viewer did not expose HTTP status, response size, or exact retrieval time, so these are not represented as raw captures.

Eight unauthenticated public page requests were made serially; no page required login or payment, and no request was retried. The 2018 result is a no-source finding, not a zero-rank series. No active provider index, catalog, or core selection was changed.
