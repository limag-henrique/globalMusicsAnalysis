# Apple Music national Top Songs 2023 — additional markets

Research note: 2026-11-17. This capture adds 2023 Apple Music national annual playlists from the 2025 additional-market scope. Markets with existing 2023 packets and markets assigned to the parallel 2020–2025 capture are excluded.

Apple Music remains provider `APPLE_MUSIC`; chart-market codes follow the named playlist title. Packets preserve published rank order, Apple song IDs and storefront track URLs, source HTML, retrieval time, and SHA-256 hashes. Scores are not published. Period dates are recorded only when the captured page states both boundaries.
