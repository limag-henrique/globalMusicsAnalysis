# Oricon Japan annual Streaming rankings, 2018–2025 — review only

Evidence reviewed: 2026-11-18. This note assesses the Oricon annual streaming-song chart family as a separate provider source. It does not alter the active provider index, catalog, selection data, Billboard JAPAN or Apple Music packets, or lyric outputs.

## Scope and official method

The chart family is Oricon’s **annual Streaming Ranking** (年間ストリーミングランキング / ストリーミングランキング), a song-consumption list. Keep it separate from Oricon’s “2025 release-year songs” subchart and from every Spotify, YouTube, Billboard JAPAN, or Apple Music ranking.

ORICON BiZ describes its digital ranking service as based on streaming-service results and says it does not make a nationwide estimate. Its current menu advertises an annual Streaming **TOP100** product, says streaming rankings start with the 2018-12-24 weekly issue, and says annual download/streaming market-trend data are available from 2019. The menu is a service description; it does not expose the year-specific paid Top100 tables used here. [ORICON BiZ menu](https://biz.oricon.co.jp/menu.asp)

JP is used as the publisher-defined Japan annual-ranking context. The method pages captured here do not give a separate calculation of listener geography; ORICON BiZ explicitly says no nationwide estimate is made.

The official weekly Streaming chart page says the current chart reviews provider-supplied plays for audio and music-video playback, including on-demand paid and ad-supported plays. It names Apple Music, Amazon Music, AWA, au Music Pass Unlimited, KKBOX, Spotify, YouTube, YouTube Music, LINE MUSIC, and Rakuten Music. This current weekly description helps identify the chart family; it does not establish an unchanged historical provider roster or a year-specific annual weighting method. [Oricon weekly Streaming chart and method](https://www.oricon.co.jp/rank/st/w/2025-12-15/)

The official annual stories label their table as a Streaming Ranking and provide the periods below. The effective period and weekly issue dates are copied only when the same first-party story prints them. No score/counts have been used to infer missing positions.

## Qualification

This evidence does **not** qualify a complete Oricon provider-year series for any year. The product menu advertises a 100-position annual chart, but the free annual evidence captures only 50 positions for 2019, ten each for 2020–2024, and four collectively stated positions for 2025. The 2018 annual streaming chart was not evidenced. The series therefore remains review-only and must not be added to the active provider index, catalog, or the Spotify/YouTube selection.

Among the recovered labels there are **no printed gaps or ties**: 2019 is contiguous 1–50, 2020–2024 are contiguous 1–10, and 2025 names the occupied set 1–4 collectively. This is a statement only about public evidence: the unrecovered tails have unknown positions and tie behavior. Across 2019–2025, 104 rank labels are recoverable (50 + five groups of ten + four), but they do not constitute seven complete year-end lists.
