## Market and methodology scope

Popnable’s [official methodology page](https://popnable.com/about) says its Tracking Bot monitors YouTube trends, views, and popularity; it links videos from an international channel to the artist’s country and videos from a local channel to the channel’s country. It describes Top 40 Songs charts as weekly, monthly, and annual. This supports identifying a separately tagged **Popnable NG trend series** (`chart_market_code=NG`) when the chart rows are verified, because the provider’s own index explicitly labels the series Nigeria/NG.

This is not evidence of listener geography or a national streaming-market audience. Popnable’s country trend may be assigned using artist or channel country, per the methodology. The current methodology page says AI technologies have been used to analyze and rank songs globally since 2022; it does not establish the exact historical method used for the 2018–2021 rows.
