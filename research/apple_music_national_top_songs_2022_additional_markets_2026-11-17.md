# Apple Music national Top Songs 2022 playlists

Research note: 2026-11-17. This capture covers the 37 national markets listed in the 2025 Apple Music collection note, excluding any 2022 packets already present or collected separately. The excluded codes were GH, ZA, IN, NG, KE, TZ, JP, PH, MY, and ID; none overlap the 37 markets in the 2025 additional-markets note.

Each ranking is an independent Apple Music provider series. Rows were taken from the Apple Music chart-market storefront page so Apple song IDs and song URLs match that storefront. Ranks were copied from Apple’s published row ranks; positions and storefront IDs were not inferred or normalized. Scores and play counts are null because the playlist pages do not publish them.
