# Japan year-end song ranking series, 2018–2025

Evidence reviewed: 2026-11-18. This additive note summarizes the qualified Apple Music and Billboard JAPAN national year-end song series in the existing provider-series packets, plus a separate Billboard JAPAN 2025 capture. It does not change the active provider-series index, selection data, catalog, earlier notes, or lyric outputs.

## Qualification and encoding

Both providers publish a Japan-labeled year-end song list, but they are distinct series:

- **Apple Music — APPLE_MUSIC, market JP, publisher country US:** official Apple Music Japan storefront playlists titled “Top Songs of [year]: Japan” (2018 uses the Japanese title “2018年トップソング100：日本”). Keep the market-storefront track IDs and the playlist’s published order.
- **Billboard JAPAN — BILLBOARD_JAPAN, market JP, publisher country JP:** official YEAR END (JAPAN) JAPAN Hot 100 rank tables. The annual index describes these as annual total-point charts. The footer identifies Hanshin Contents Link Corporation as publisher under license from Billboard Media, LLC; its [company profile](https://hcl-c.com/english/company-info.html) lists its headquarters in Osaka.

The provider code, chart family, reporting year, and published position identify every row. These rankings are not Spotify annual charts or YouTube annual charts. They remain separate from those providers and from each other; no cross-provider deduplication or placement into the 150,000-position Spotify/YouTube selection is justified.

## Completeness, ties, and gaps

- **16 qualified provider-year series:** Apple Music Japan 2018–2025 (8), and Billboard JAPAN Hot 100 2018–2025 (8).
- **1,600 provider-specific ranked memberships:** 100 per series. This is not a count of unique songs across providers.
- **Published depth:** 100 for each series. Recovered rank range: 1–100 for each.
- **Ties/gaps:** no tied rank labels, missing position labels, or duplicate positions are published in the captured series. No rank positions remain unresolved.
- **Unresolved metadata:** exact measurement windows and numeric playback/point totals are not available in the cited pages. Billboard coverage is stated by month; Apple pages are year-labeled playlists.

## Review-only boundaries

- Billboard JAPAN’s 2025 year-end index visibly separates **Japan** and **World** navigation and lists multiple chart families. This packet qualifies only JAPAN Hot 100; World charts, albums, artist charts, and genre-specific lists such as Hot Animation are not substitute rows for a national all-songs series.
- Apple Music’s source is a year-labeled Japan storefront playlist, not a Billboard chart or a Spotify/YouTube annual list. The 2018 page’s publication timestamp is not used as a measurement window.
- No unsupported or partially observed rank has been promoted into either series. There are no review-only positions; the remaining review-only exclusions are other markets, providers, and chart categories described above.
