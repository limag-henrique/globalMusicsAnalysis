# East Africa annual music ranking sources: KE, TZ, ET (2018–2025)

Date consulted: 2026-11-16. This note assesses first-party chart-publisher sources for Kenya (KE), Tanzania (TZ), and Ethiopia (ET) during 2018–2025, where the main Spotify/YouTube annual cohort currently lacks verified coverage. Each publisher's positions must remain a distinct provider series; do not merge or substitute ranks across providers.

## Popnable year-end Top 40 (all three markets)

Popnable's Kenya, Tanzania, and Ethiopia chart archives each list year-end pages for every year from 2018 through 2025. The individual 2024 and 2025 chart pages identify the period as January 1 through December 31 of the named year and label the output a Top 40; the 2025 pages show ranked entries and pagination. These are publisher-issued annual lists, distinct from Spotify Charts, official YouTube Charts, Apple Music, and Ethio.Music rankings. [Kenya archive](https://popnable.com/kenya/charts/top-40) · [Tanzania archive](https://popnable.com/tanzania/charts/top-40) · [Ethiopia archive](https://popnable.com/ethiopia/charts/top-40) · [Kenya 2025](https://popnable.com/kenya/charts/top-40/year-2025) · [Tanzania 2025](https://popnable.com/tanzania/charts/top-40/year-2025) · [Ethiopia 2025](https://popnable.com/ethiopia/charts/top-40/year-2025).

There is an unresolved denominator conflict in Popnable's own descriptions. The Kenya and Tanzania country-profile pages say the annual chart is a Top 500, while the annual chart pages and Popnable's general methodology call it a Top 40. Until the publisher's actual annual page structure and complete row count are checked, treat Popnable as a source-published annual-chart candidate, but do not import it as a verified 40-position series or assume a 40-entry denominator. [Kenya profile](https://popnable.com/kenya) · [Tanzania profile](https://popnable.com/tanzania) · [Popnable chart methodology](https://popnable.com/about).

Popnable says its charts use data collected by its tracking bot, which monitors YouTube trends, views, and popularity; it also considers regional trends and has used AI to analyze and rank songs since 2022. Its methodology describes an annual Top 40 format, but does not publish a year-specific coverage audit, an aggregation formula, or missing-data treatment. The year pages are not explicitly marked provisional, but the complete chart size and measurement coverage remain unverified from the source. Preserve any later captured positions under provider `Popnable`; do not label them Spotify or official YouTube rankings. [Popnable chart methodology](https://popnable.com/about).

The date window and list label are explicit on representative year pages: [Kenya 2024](https://popnable.com/kenya/charts/top-40/year-2024), [Tanzania 2024](https://popnable.com/tanzania/charts/top-40/year-2024), and [Ethiopia 2024](https://popnable.com/ethiopia/charts/top-40/year-2024) each state 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31; the 2025 pages state 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31. The archive pages show the contiguous 2018–2025 year-end entries for all three markets. Individual historical pages and the full pagination should be checked at capture time because of the denominator conflict and undisclosed source-data gaps.

## Apple Music Kenya Top Songs (2024–2025)

Apple Music publishes a 100-song Kenya end-of-year playlist for 2024 and another for 2025. It states that these charts are based on plays from November 1 of the preceding year through October 31 of the named year, so these are rolling Apple Music year-end periods, not calendar-year charts. The pages expose an ordered playlist but no numeric play totals; preserve Apple Music as its own provider series with null scores and the publisher's stated periods. [Top Songs of 2024: Kenya](https://music.apple.com/ke/playlist/top-songs-of-2024-kenya/pl.430ae3ccdd2d46d88b23c8af4519c7db) · [Top Songs of 2025: Kenya](https://music.apple.com/ke/playlist/top-songs-of-2025-kenya/pl.39eb6078811543b4acc4cb334bdabcb7).

No additional Apple Music Kenya annual playlists for 2018–2023 were verified in this review. That is a search result, not proof that such pages never existed.

## Ethiopia: existing Ethio.Music provisional list

Ethio.Music's current chart index lists the Ethiopian Music Top 40 of 2025 as its yearly chart; its methodology page otherwise describes its weekly boards and monthly Top 10. The captured 2025 annual page is explicitly provisional and says its total is reconstructed from Kworb's YouTube Ethiopia weekly Top 20 across 29 recoverable 2025 chart weeks. It therefore is not a complete-year measurement and remains separate from Popnable's own year-end page. [Ethio.Music Charts](https://ethio.music/charts) · [Ethiopian Music Top 40 of 2025](https://ethio.music/138) · [Methodology](https://ethio.music/methodology).

## Reviewed but not eligible

Ospo Charts describes a planned East African weekly BIG 40, quarterly BIG 100, and year-end BIG 200, but its publisher page calls the interface a concept preview with no live data and says the first official ranking has not been calculated. It is not an available annual chart for these years. [Ospo Charts](https://ospo.tz/sw/charts).

Mdundo's Kenya Top 100 page reviewed here is explicitly a weekly chart, so it does not establish an annual series. No annual Kenya list was verified from that page. [Mdundo Kenya weekly Top 100](https://mdundo.com/best/ke/64).

## Addendum (2026-11-17): Popnable 2025 annual-page pagination

Page-level review of the 2025 annual charts confirms that the chart URL's “Top 40” label does not establish a 40-row annual depth. The [Kenya 2025 page](https://popnable.com/kenya/charts/top-40/year-2025) shows ranks 1–20 and its next page shows 21–40. The [Ethiopia 2025 page](https://popnable.com/ethiopia/charts/top-40/year-2025) shows ranks 1–20, page 2 shows 21–40, and page 3 shows 41–60. The [Tanzania 2025 page](https://popnable.com/tanzania/charts/top-40/year-2025) exposes ranks 1–20 and a next-page link, but the page-2 request returned a server error during this check. The chart pages identify the period as January 1–December 31, 2025.

Popnable's [Kenya country profile](https://popnable.com/kenya) and [Ethiopia country profile](https://popnable.com/ethiopia) describe yearly charts as Top 500, while the general [chart methodology](https://popnable.com/about) describes Top 40 songs charts as weekly, monthly, and annual. This agrees with the earlier Kenya/Tanzania profile-depth conflict and adds Ethiopia to the unresolved set. The Ethiopia page's ranks through 60 establish that its annual page extends beyond 40, but the final available position and complete year-specific depth were not verified. Do not infer a 40- or 500-entry denominator from these partial checks.

The pages were reviewed through web text extraction. Direct local page requests returned HTTP 403, so no raw HTML/hash packet was created and no ranking rows were imported. Before importing any of these series, recover the full page chain and verify the last published rank and source body for each year/market.
