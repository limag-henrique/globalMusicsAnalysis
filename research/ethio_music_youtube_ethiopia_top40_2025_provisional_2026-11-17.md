# Ethio.Music YouTube Ethiopia Top 40 (2025, provisional)

Date consulted: 2026-11-17 UTC. This is a separate publisher series for Ethiopia. It is not merged with the official YouTube charts or the 150,000-position Spotify/YouTube selection.

## Published scope and limitations

Ethio.Music labels its yearly chart for 2025 as provisional. Its method sums each song's views from Kworb's YouTube Ethiopia weekly Top 20 across the 29 chart weeks recovered from Internet Archive snapshots. The other weeks were not estimated. The publisher warns that songs outside the weekly Top 20 and songs peaking during unavailable weeks are undercounted, especially lower positions. It excludes titles it does not classify as Ethiopian. This is therefore a captured provisional ranking, not complete-year evidence.

The page publishes 40 consecutive positions and a numeric total for each song. The totals and weekly appearance/peak details are preserved. Source upload classification is retained as publisher text and is not treated as an independent identity or rights verification.
