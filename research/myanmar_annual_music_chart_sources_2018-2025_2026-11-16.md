# Myanmar annual music chart sources (2018–2025)

Date consulted: 2026-11-16 (America/Sao_Paulo). This note reviews first-party Myanmar chart publishers and platforms for annual ranked song lists during the project's high-gap period. A source counts only if the publisher exposes an annual song list with meaningful song positions. Award winners, nominations, radio program names, and daily or weekly charts do not supply those annual positions.

## Findings

| Market | Verified annual ranked song lists | Years | Positions acquired |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Myanmar (MM) | None found in the reviewed first-party sources | 2018–2025 | 0 |

No eligible song positions can be acquired from this review. The MM annual-ranking gap remains for every year from 2018 through 2025. No alternative order was inferred from award categories or other non-annual material.

## Reviewed first-party sources

### Yangon City FM / YCDC

Yangon City Development Committee (YCDC) maintains a [City FM anniversary awards archive](https://www.ycdc.gov.mm/CityFMAward) and a separate [City FM anniversary songs archive](https://ycdc.gov.mm/CityFMSong). The awards archive includes year-specific results for 2018 ([17th anniversary](https://www.ycdc.gov.mm/CityFMAward/3)), 2019 ([18th anniversary](https://www.ycdc.gov.mm/CityFMAward/2)), and 2020 ([19th anniversary](https://www.ycdc.gov.mm/CityFMAward/1)). These pages report winners by categories such as best-selling album producer, best-selling vocalist, and most requested or popular vocalist. They do not publish a ranked annual song list or song-level positions, so no rows were taken from them.

The City FM anniversary songs index lists anniversary-year pages through its 19th anniversary, but the linked detail pages repeatedly timed out during this review. Their contents could not be checked for a ranked song list; the index alone does not expose song positions. This remains an unresolved official lead.

### Shwe FM

Shwe FM's official [daily program schedule](https://shwefmradio.net/content/daily-program) includes a radio slot named “Shwe Top 10 Series.” The schedule gives no ranked songs, annual label, or annual archive. It confirms a Top 10 radio program, not a recoverable year-end list.

### JOOX Myanmar

JOOX announced that its Myanmar music service would end on 6 July 2022 in its [official notice](https://www.joox.com/mm/article/4490). The first-party [Myanmar Top 100 By JOOX playlist](https://www.joox.com/mm/playlist/nnvy9szQAWXszmBR%2B6v4QA%3D%3D) currently reports zero songs. The playlist is not labeled as a year-end chart, and no JOOX Myanmar annual song ranking with positions was verified for 2018–2022. The service shutdown makes it a historical lead, but no positions were recoverable from the reviewed pages.

### Apple Music

Apple's [Top Songs of 2025 index](https://music.apple.com/mm/room/6755978957) lists country and regional end-of-year playlists for multiple markets, but the reviewed index exposes no Myanmar-specific Top Songs playlist. This is a 2025 storefront check only; it does not prove that no historical Myanmar playlist ever existed. No Myanmar-specific Apple Music annual list was verified for 2018–2025.

### YouTube Music Charts

Google's [YouTube Charts help page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9014376?hl=en) describes daily and weekly Top Songs charts and lists the 61 supported chart markets; Myanmar is not listed. This is evidence that YouTube does not offer a Myanmar country chart in the reviewed chart system, and that the system described is daily/weekly rather than an annual Myanmar list.

### Other annual awards lead

Major M's [2018 Music Awards page](https://majormmyanmar.wordpress.com/major-m-music-awards/) lists song-of-the-year winners and nominees by award category. It is an awards result, not a ranked annual chart: nominee order is not a published performance ranking, and no chart positions are assigned. It was therefore reviewed but not counted.

## Year coverage and exact gap

| Years | Primary-source evidence reviewed | Remaining gap |
|---:|---|---|
| 2018–2020 | City FM annual award results; a 2018 Major M awards page | Awards identify category winners/nominees but publish no annual song positions. City FM's separate anniversary-song detail pages could not be fetched. |

| 2023–2025 | Apple Music 2025 year-end index; current Shwe FM schedule; YouTube chart-market list | No Myanmar-specific annual ranked song list found. Shwe FM's Top 10 program exposes no year-end positions; Myanmar is absent from YouTube's supported chart markets. |

The reviewed primary sources yield **zero annual song positions** for MM in 2018–2025. Any future recovery from the City FM anniversary-song pages should first establish the page's year, whether the order is explicitly a ranking, and the complete number of entries before importing positions.
