## Source and market notes

TME's official [UNI Chart rules](https://www.tencentmusic.com/zh-cn/uni-chart.html) describe its chart as tracking Chinese-language songs and combining data from QQ Music, Kugou, Kuwo, Bodian Music, JOOX, Tencent Video, and Sina Weibo. The currently published rules define a language/service scope and scoring inputs, not a listener-geography restriction. These current rules should not be retroactively treated as the exact 2018–2020 methodology. TME's [official annual-report archive](https://chart.tencentmusic.com/) currently surfaces 2020 and later annual-report material, but the reviewed archive did not surface 2018 or 2019 annual pages. Its 2020 archive entry resolved to an image rather than ranked rows. Record the publisher as TME/CN and leave chart market unset unless a year-specific primary source establishes it.

QQ Music is also a Tencent service, so CN describes the publisher jurisdiction. The [2019 year-end page](https://y.qq.com/m/act/dfb2019/index.html) and [2020 year-end page](https://y.qq.com/m/act/dfb2020/index.html) provide useful machine-readable source URLs and full selections by category, but their published list order is explicitly not rank order. Neither page establishes that the chart's audience or eligible listening activity is mainland-only. Keep any QQ annual selection distinct from TME UNI and CAVCA; do not assign it a mainland chart-market code from the publisher location alone.

CAVCA's [official chart launch announcement](https://www.cavca.org/newsDetail/1412) says Golden Mic uses offline KTV on-demand data from CAVCA's copyright big-data platform, calls it a national karaoke chart, and describes monthly Hot Songs and New Songs categories. CAVCA's [official annual-chart timeline](https://www.cavca.org/newsDetail/1619) says the first annual chart was published at the end of 2021; the [first annual list post](https://www.cavca.org/newsDetail/1620) is dated 2022-01-01 and covers that first annual chart. This is evidence for a CN karaoke market, but it does not fill 2018–2020.

## Gaps and encoding recommendation

- No rank-ordered, first-party annual song ranking for mainland China in 2018–2020 was verified in this research pass.
- QQ Music's [2019](https://y.qq.com/m/act/dfb2019/index.html) and [2020](https://y.qq.com/m/act/dfb2020/index.html) year-end pages are useful source artifacts for complete annual selections, but they are not rank tables: the pages disclaim rank order and supply no row-level rank or score. Do not extract their displayed sequence as positions.
- TME's [2020 archive entry](https://chart.tencentmusic.com/) is not enough to establish an annual ranked list; no ranked row page was recovered. No 2018 or 2019 UNI annual source was found in the reviewed official archive.
- CAVCA's [launch announcement](https://www.cavca.org/newsDetail/1412) and [annual-chart timeline](https://www.cavca.org/newsDetail/1619) show the series launched in December 2020 and its first annual list was for 2021, leaving 2018–2020 empty.
- The 2018 QQ candidate route `https://y.qq.com/m/act/dfb2018/index.html` returned not found when checked; this only documents that route, not proof that no other first-party 2018 chart page exists.

If the project retains non-ranked annual selections, represent the QQ pages as separate provider/category records with `publisher_country=CN`, `market_scope=not stated`, year, category, published list depth, and source URL, while leaving rank null. For ranked annual-chart cells, leave 2018–2020 unfilled until an ordered primary-source list with a verifiable ranking method and market scope is found.

## Additional first-party route checked (2026-11-17)

The official [TME UNI Chart milestone page](https://yobang.tencentmusic.com/chart/uni-chart/milestone/) reports 423 chart issues and 242 champion songs, and displays records for songs with consecutive weekly wins. Its note says the page is ordered by achievement score, with ties ordered by the time the achievement was reached. This is an all-time consecutive-win record board, not an annual song ranking, so its display order does not provide annual positions and no rows were imported.

A follow-up search for the Tencent Entertainment 2018 White Paper surfaced only secondary reproductions of its claims; no primary ranked annual-song table or first-party report artifact was verified in this pass. Do not encode the secondary retellings as chart positions.
