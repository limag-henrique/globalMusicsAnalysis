# Bangladesh annual chart source review: BD-2022 and BD-2024

**Consulted:** 2026-11-19. **Scope:** first-party national chart candidates for BD-2022 and BD-2024; whether the existing Spotify BD-2023 five-track list can be treated as a separate partial provider series. Research only: no database/cache or provider packet was touched.

## Finding

Popnable publishes a Bangladesh **Top 40 Songs, Year-End** page for both 2022 and 2024, and each page explicitly states the calendar interval **01/01–31/12** for the named year. These are first-party Popnable chart pages and can be considered separate provider-defined Bangladesh series, subject to the limitations below. ([BD-2022 year-end chart](https://popnable.com/bangladesh/charts/top-40/year-2022); [BD-2024 year-end chart](https://popnable.com/bangladesh/charts/top-40/year-2024))

The pages show ordinal chart positions (Bangladesh #1, #2, etc.) and describe the lists as Top 40. Popnable’s methodology page says Top 40 Songs has weekly, monthly, and annual charts. The 2024 insights page explicitly references positions 39 and 40, corroborating 40-place depth for that issue. No numeric score is published on the year-end chart pages. ([Popnable methodology](https://popnable.com/about); [2024 chart insights](https://popnable.com/bangladesh/charts/top-40/year-2024/insights))

**Geography and methodology are provider-defined.** Popnable’s Bangladesh chart index calls these local charts and says its Top 40 lists songs “from Bangladesh.” Its methodology says the Tracking Bot monitors YouTube trends, views, and popularity, with regional influences and AI analysis since 2022; it links international-channel videos to an artist’s country and local-channel videos to the channel’s country. This does not establish listener location or a Bangladesh-only listening denominator. Popnable’s own terms add that ranking pages use its editorial evaluation and may not relate to data derived by video platforms. The appropriate description is a Popnable Bangladesh trend chart, not an independently verified national consumption chart or a platform stream-count ranking. ([Bangladesh chart index](https://popnable.com/bangladesh/charts); [methodology](https://popnable.com/about); [terms](https://popnable.com/terms))

Popnable’s LinkedIn company profile self-reports its headquarters and primary location as London, GB. This supports recording publisher location as profile-reported **GB**, not a verified legal domicile. ([Popnable company profile](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/popnable))

## BD-2023 Spotify boundary

Spotify’s official 2023 Wrapped Mapped article and embedded chart identify a panel titled **Top Songs in Bangladesh** with five numbered tracks. The article describes local streaming trends since January 1, but does not state a period end date or a detailed ranking formula. Thus this is a five-entry Spotify/BD provider list with incomplete period coverage evidence; preserve it as its own partial provider series. Do not fill positions 6–40 or combine its ranks with Popnable. Spotify’s 2022 year-end article and 2024 media kit list global and U.S. top-song charts, not a Bangladesh annual chart; these do not extend the Spotify series to BD-2022 or BD-2024. ([Spotify 2023 article](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2023-11-29/wrapped-top-songs-worldwide-map/); [official interactive map](https://newsroom.spotify.com/wp-content/themes/ftr/assets/animations/wrapped2023/WRAPPEDMAPPEDV8/WRAPPEDMAPPEDV8.html); [Spotify 2022 year-end article](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2022-11-30/the-top-songs-artists-podcasts-and-listening-trends-of-2022/); [Spotify 2024 Wrapped media kit](https://newsroom.spotify.com/media-kit/2024-wrapped/))
