# ACUM Israel radio Top 20 for 2025: source verification

Audit date: 2026-11-17.

## Source and scope

ACUM's year-end release is titled “אק״ם חושפת את מצעד השירים והמוזיקאים המושמעים ביותר בתחנות הרדיו הישראליות בשנת 2025” (“ACUM reveals the most-played songs and musicians on Israeli radio stations in 2025”). ACUM's news archive dates the release 2025-12-25. The first-party PDF says AcumOnAir monitored 34 licensed national and regional Israeli radio stations. Its table is headed “20 most-played works on radio stations (all stations combined)” and gives positions 1–20. The report does not publish per-song play counts or a ranking formula.

## Source conflict and capture hashes

The report's narrative on PDF page 1 calls the work at rank 4 “פרחים”, while the ranked table on page 3 prints “פרפרים”. The packet preserves the table title and records this internal discrepancy; it does not infer an alternate song identity.

The live ACUM file URL currently returns an HTML page rather than a PDF. That failed response remains at [the original direct-fetch file](../data/raw/national_charts/acum_il_2025/acum-on-air-israel-radio-top-20-2025.pdf); it is HTML despite its suffix and has SHA-256 86acb909baff772ab6e89b36ab3053c681540fc704a51fce0506f41a0289a81e.

The Internet Archive CDX lists the archived source as HTTP 200, application/pdf, captured at 2026-01-17T16:48:52Z, with payload digest VYX7WOPW6KGAFWG2W756XGNAPZLFMQJR (base32 SHA-1). The downloaded PDF's SHA-1 base32 matches that digest. Its local copy is [the verified archived PDF](../data/raw/national_charts/acum_il_2025/acum-on-air-israel-radio-top-20-2025-wayback-20260117.pdf), SHA-256 b9bba024bb72f85a68d71123c0bc84df20e0cdbfed3df1904498718ee2a677b2. I rendered pages 1, 3, and 4 and visually verified the heading, scope, positions, and all 20 table rows.
