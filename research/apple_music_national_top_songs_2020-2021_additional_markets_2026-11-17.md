## Capture and validation

- Retrieved the playlist pages through their matching chart-market storefronts from 2026-11-17 04:16:36 UTC through 04:27:53 UTC. Apple Music may resolve the same playlist to different song IDs by storefront, so each packet keeps only the local storefront page and local catalog IDs.
- Extracted the 100 displayed track rows in published order from Apple Music's serialized page data. The source page, `rankings.jsonl`, and manifest are retained in each `apple-music-{market}-{year}-annual` directory.
- Independently checked all 37 packets: page and ranking SHA-256 values and byte counts; Apple playlist ID and market storefront; page song-count metadata; the 1–100 rank sequence; each row's provider, market, local storefront, native song ID, source hash, and non-merger flag. All checks passed.
- None of these pages stated exact aggregation start and end dates. `period_start` and `period_end` are therefore null in every packet.
- Japan 2021 was already captured in the existing series and was excluded here to avoid duplicate series. This set is an additional acquisition, not an exhaustive inventory of Apple Music country-year pages.
