# CAVCA Golden Mic annual song rankings (2021–2025)

Date consulted: 2026-11-16 (America/Sao_Paulo). This note records annual rankings from the China Audio-Video Copyright Association (CAVCA) as separate provider series. Golden Mic is based on offline KTV on-demand data in China, not Spotify or YouTube streams.

## Provider, market, and method

CAVCA describes Golden Mic (金麦榜) as a karaoke chart based on offline KTV on-demand data in China; it launched in December 2020. CAVCA distinguishes a monthly popular-singles chart (热歌榜), based on monthly karaoke requests, and a monthly new-singles chart (新歌榜), for songs released within one year of the monthly chart. The inspected annual reports/posts do not disclose the exact annual aggregation formula or precise start/end dates. The artifacts preserve the annual label, published ordinal positions, chart category, titles, performers, and source evidence; dates and scores remain null where the source does not state them.

The market code is CN because CAVCA identifies the series as a Chinese national offline-KTV chart. Each category/year has its own series ID and denominator. No positions were merged into the 150,000-position Spotify/YouTube selection. CAVCA's 2024 report says its monitoring platform covered 28 provinces/autonomous regions/municipalities, 167 cities, 1,515 karaoke venues and 33,000 rooms, equal to 11.9% of CAVCA-licensed venues. This is a partial venue sample, not a complete census.

The chart began in December 2020 and its first annual list was published for 2021. The 2026 annual year is incomplete as of this research date.

## Local evidence

Each year has an additive packet at data/derived/annual_revision/provider_series/cavca-golden-mic-cn-<year>-annual/. The rankings JSONL has one row per position and a separate series ID for each year/category. The manifest records source URLs, local hashes, retrieval times, market scope, rank depth, and limitations. The packets do not imply lyric import, classification approval, Vertex eligibility, or upload.
