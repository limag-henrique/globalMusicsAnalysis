# Nigeria year-end song rankings: TurnTable Charts, 2018–2021

Research note: 2026-11-16. Scope is first-party publisher evidence for Nigeria-focused annual song rankings, with TurnTable Charts (TTC) as the principal national chart publisher reviewed. This note records only rank positions stated in TTC-owned pages; it does not reconstruct ranks from weekly peaks or secondary reporting.

## Findings

For the 2022–2024 follow-up, see [Nigeria year-end song rankings: TurnTable Charts, 2022–2024](nigeria_turntable_year_end_song_rankings_2022-2024_2026-11-17.md).

- **2018–2019:** No TurnTable year-end song ranking is supported. TTC says its chart tracking began in 2020; the first weekly chart was the Top Airplay Chart (9 July 2020), followed by the Top Streaming Chart (13 July), TV Top Songs (7 September), and the first aggregate TurnTable Top 50 (11 November). This establishes the start of TTC’s chart series, not the nonexistence of every possible Nigerian annual list. [TTC history and methodology](https://www.turntablecharts.com/news/480)
- **2020:** TTC published *End of the Year Charts 2020* on 18 December 2020. TTC’s later comparison calls the 2020 and 2025 annual song rankings “Year-End Top 50” charts, so 50 is the documented 2020 chart depth. The original page is an image-heavy compilation; its page text directs readers to the newsletter for the full annual charts and Top 50. [2020 year-end page](https://www.turntablecharts.com/magazine/1st/13), [2020 vs. 2025 Top 50 comparison](https://www.turntablecharts.com/news/2075)
- **2021:** TTC published *End of the Year Charts 2021* on 21 December 2021 and describes it as a compilation of top songs across platforms, genres, charts, and the overall year. A contemporaneous TTC analysis refers to an “End of the Year Top 50” and “overall Top 50,” but it also assigns “Your Body” rank 70 on the Year-End Chart. The exact depth is therefore internally inconsistent in TTC’s accessible text; do not label the 2021 overall list as exactly 50 entries without resolving the No. 70 reference. Its annual page consists of embedded images with no ranking rows in extracted page text. [2021 year-end page](https://www.turntablecharts.com/magazine/2nd/31), [2021 chart analysis](https://www.turntablecharts.com/magazine/2nd/20)

## Method and denominator limits

1. TTC’s year-end pages confirm annual compilations, but their extracted text does not publish a full rank table. The 2020 page points to newsletter access for the full chart; the 2021 page embeds graphics. The linked 2020/2021 graphics were not retrievable during this audit, so the complete rank order cannot be captured from the currently accessible page text.
2. Do not substitute weekly chart ranks for annual positions. The 2021 analysis separately discusses weekly TurnTable Top 50 peaks (for example, songs topping the weekly chart) and year-end ranks; only the explicit year-end rank statements above are recorded.
3. Do not transfer the later weekly Top 100 formula to 2020/2021. TTC says the 2020 aggregate TurnTable Top 50 was its first combined streaming/radio/TV weekly chart, launched 11 November 2020. TTC’s detailed weighted formula belongs to the new Nigeria Top 100, launched July 2022. TTC’s 2022 recap says the 2022 Year-End Charts combined streaming with airplay (radio and TV), but the accessible 2020/2021 annual-page text does not state an equivalent weighting formula or complete denominator. [TTC chart history and 2022 methodology](https://www.turntablecharts.com/news/480), [2022 Year-End Top 100 recap](https://www.turntablecharts.com/news/697)
4. TTC’s 2021 text both says “Top 50” and reports an annual song at No. 70. Record the contradiction as unresolved instead of assuming a 50- or 100-entry denominator.

## Source inventory

All evidence cited above is from TurnTable Charts’ own pages. No secondary ranking reconstruction was used.

## Provider-specific packet capture

The recoverable positions were captured on 2026-11-17 UTC as separate TurnTable Charts series:

- `TURNTABLE_CHARTS_YEAR_END_SONGS_NG_2020`: ranks 1, 2, 9, and 12; the publisher describes the chart as a Top 50.
- `TURNTABLE_CHARTS_YEAR_END_SONGS_NG_2021`: ranks 1, 2, 3, 8, 10–14, 33, 34, and 70; the exact chart depth remains unresolved because the source text says both “Top 50” and rank 70.

The local packets preserve only explicit year-end ranks and include the annual page plus the TTC articles supporting each row. Missing rows were not reconstructed from weekly charts or unavailable graphics. Both series remain provider-specific and separate from Spotify/YouTube rankings.

## Internet Archive follow-up (2026-11-17)

Wayback snapshots confirm the first-party pages were captured, but they do not currently resolve the missing overall rank tables. The 2020 `YearlyTop50` route snapshot contains the chart-page shell and title but no song rows in its captured HTML ([2020 chart-route snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20201230181738/https://www.turntablecharts.com/Charts/YearlyTop50)). The archived 2020 article still directs readers to subscribe to the TTC newsletter for the full annual charts and Top 50 ([2020 article snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20211022065407/http://turntablecharts.com/Photo/Post/7/End-of-the-Year-Charts-2020)).

The archived 2021 article confirms the publisher's description of the compilation as top songs across platforms, genres, charts, and the overall year ([2021 article snapshot](https://web.archive.org/web/20211228160954/https://www.turntablecharts.com/mag/31/End-of-the-Year-Charts-2021)). Some Cloudinary graphics referenced by that page were recovered from Wayback after their live URLs returned 404; the reviewed examples are award-category cards such as “Top Song by Emerging Artiste (Female),” not a complete ordered overall ranking. The sparse TTC positions above therefore remain the only rows imported; no ranks were inferred from the replicated secondary lists.
