# Apple Music national annual Top Songs: Philippines, Malaysia, Japan, and Indonesia (2020–2025)

Date consulted: 2026-11-17 UTC. This note records official Apple Music year-end playlist series as provider-separated rankings. The playlist country label supplies chart market; Apple Music publisher jurisdiction is recorded separately as `publisher_country_code=US`.

## Market-storefront ID selection

The rankings use Apple Music song IDs and recording URLs resolved through each chart-market storefront (`/ph`, `/my`, `/jp`, `/id`). The earlier `/us` source page is preserved in every packet as `source-page-us.html` and listed as a secondary artifact; it does not supply ranked native IDs. The local serialized playlist order and native IDs agree with each page’s JSON-LD track array, and count/ordinal sequence match the corresponding `/us` capture. No fallback or position/count gap was needed. Local storefront title and artist-credit strings are retained where they differ.

## Local provider packets

Each verified year is stored in `data/derived/annual_revision/provider_series/apple-music-{ph|my|jp|id}-<year>-annual/` with the chart-market storefront page as `source-page.html`, the preserved US page as `source-page-us.html`, a manifest, and ordered rankings. Each year remains a separate Apple Music series with its own 100-position denominator; no ranking is merged into Spotify, YouTube, or another provider. The provider-series index and supplemental catalog were not modified.

The Philippines 2020 and 2021 pages remain unresolved; no series packet was created for those years.
