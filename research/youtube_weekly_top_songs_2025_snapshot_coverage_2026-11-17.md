# YouTube Weekly Top Songs: 2025 Snapshot Coverage Audit

Audit date: 2026-11-17. This reviews the structured snapshots in [`data/raw/annual_top80/20261021`](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/) and the v10 core country-year ledger. Counts below are from enumerating archived JSON records whose `period_end` is in 2025; no rankings or source packets were changed.

## Coverage

Using the archive's `NATIVE_WEEK_END_YEAR` convention, the 2025 calendar contains 52 Thursday week-ending dates, 2025-01-02 through 2025-12-25. There are 2,630 snapshots across 52 markets: 50 markets have all 52 weeks; DK has 29 (23 missing) and NO has 1 (51 missing). The complete markets are AE, AR, AT, AU, BE, BO, BR, CA, CH, CL, CO, CR, CZ, DE, DO, EC, EG, ES, FI, FR, GB, GT, HN, HU, ID, IE, IL, IN, IT, JP, KR, MX, NI, NL, NZ, PA, PE, PL, PT, PY, RO, RU, SA, SE, SV, TR, UA, US, UY, ZA. The overall collection manifest is marked `PARTIAL`; that status covers the larger 94-week collection, while these counts describe the 2025 subset. ([inventory manifest](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/inventory_manifest.json); [collection manifest](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/collection_manifest.json); [sample IL snapshot](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/YOUTUBE_CHARTS_WEB_IL_2025-01-02_2af7da453a7b.json))

These are complete *week-ending-year* snapshots, not day-aligned calendar-year totals: the first 2025 chart period runs 2024-12-27–2025-01-02 and the last runs 2025-12-19–2025-12-25. Aggregating their weekly metrics therefore follows the archive's native week convention rather than exactly covering Jan 1–Dec 31. ## Provenance and ranking fields

Records identify `YOUTUBE_CHARTS_WEB` / `YOUTUBE_MUSIC` / `WEEKLY_TOP_SONGS`, the market, period, source URL, observed market, retrieval timestamp, and rows. URLs use YouTube's official `charts.youtube.com/charts/TopSongs/{market}/weekly/{date}` route; the sample IL capture was retrieved 2026-10-24. Rows include `rank`, `source_rank`, `title`, `artist`, `native_id`, and numeric `metric_value`. The archive's availability manifest marks weekly views as verified, and YouTube's documentation says Weekly Top Songs ranks views across official music videos, user videos using the official song, and lyric videos. These are weekly chart observations from which a provider-specific annual series could be derived; they are not themselves an official YouTube year-end chart. ([sample IL snapshot](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/YOUTUBE_CHARTS_WEB_IL_2025-01-02_2af7da453a7b.json); [source availability](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/source_availability.json); [YouTube Charts & Insights](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9014376?hl=en))

## Row quality and core gaps

Across the 2,600 snapshots in the 50 complete markets (259,982 rows), row depth is 99–101 per chart. All rows have a title, rank, and numeric metric; 268 have a blank artist. There are 24 `missing_title_positions` entries and no repeated `rank` values; however, 165 rows have `source_rank` different from `rank` (164 by one position), so those fields should not be treated as interchangeable. There are 289 same-week repeated-`native_id` groups (290 excess rows); each group keeps the same title/artist but repeats them at different ranks and metric values, so weekly observations need duplicate handling before metric aggregation. IDs are `web-derived:` hashes rather than YouTube video IDs, and the hashes did not map to conflicting title/artist pairs in this audit. ([2025 snapshot archive](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/); [sample IL snapshot](../data/raw/annual_top80/20261021/YOUTUBE_CHARTS_WEB_IL_2025-01-02_2af7da453a7b.json))

Comparing the 50 full-snapshot markets with the v10 core ledger, 48 cells already meet their core quota and none are partially filled. IL is empty against quota 68 and RU is empty against quota 268; both are `NO_VERIFIED_ANNUAL_SOURCE`. DK and NO are quota-filled in the core from Spotify despite their incomplete YouTube snapshot calendars. This makes IL and RU candidates for evaluating a separate, explicitly YouTube-tagged 2025 series, subject to duplicate and annual aggregation rules; this audit did not add any memberships.
