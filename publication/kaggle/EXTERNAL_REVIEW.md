## Suggested review tasks

1. Verify hashes and uniqueness before analysis.
2. Recompute annual and pooled denominators from distinct recording membership.
3. Compare country weighting with distinct-recording weighting.
4. Inspect source/chart-family sensitivity and missing annotation coverage.
5. Reproduce exact-year contextual correlations with recorded sample sizes.
6. Reproduce all 30 search models and the full BH correction family.
7. Obtain a lawful lyric version, compare its hash and inspect the coding criteria.
8. Distinguish textual stance from mention, narrator, artist and listener behavior.

Do not place copyrighted full lyrics, secrets or personal credentials in a public
issue. Cite the recording/source and describe the discrepancy with concise evidence.
